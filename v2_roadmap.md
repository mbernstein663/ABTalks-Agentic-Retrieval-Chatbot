# Future Roadmap

What does this chatbot really need to take it to the next level?

## Future implementation:

- **Direct TOS Citations**: Show actual chunks, not just citation labels for absolute clarity
- **Non-Stale Plan+ Claim Info**: Plan + claim lookup on the side, allows for automatic information retrieval without ever asking the bot
- **Voice Dictation** for long-winded explanations or complex searches
- **Rate Limiting** and model-swapping to eliminate costs for long conversations

### Real-World Implementation

- **Cloud Kubernetes Service** to maintain on cloud rather than local server
- **Compliance Review**: accuracy, ensuring not overstepping with claims and advice, not referencing stale documents/databases
- **Frontend Boost**: Make the frontend look nicer and more professional before releasing. Swap from Streamlit to something more customizable like React.
- **Stronger LLM**: Use frontier OpenAI or Claude models to increase performance and reasoning.
- **Non-keyword mapping**: semantic guardrails rather than keyword matching for PII violations or prompt injections.
